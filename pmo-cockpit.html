The problem

Critical decisions are buried in conversation.

During fast-paced meetings, decisions, technical constraints and task assignments are buried inside unstructured human conversation. Traditional project tools rely on someone parsing that conversation and typing it into a tracker after the fact. That delayed, manual data entry introduces information latency — by the time the system is updated, the project's actual state has already moved on.

How it works

Three successive computational layers.

Instead of a passive repository, the cockpit runs as an active agent. Information moves through three layers — extraction, validation and telemetry — before it reaches your dashboard.

01

Extraction

Capture the conversation and structure it.

  • Data enters via direct API integration or the AI note-taker that records meeting transcripts.
  • NLP generates an overarching summary and isolates actionable items from the surrounding talk.
  • Action items are bound to specific attendees.
  • Historical reconciliation cross-references prior backlogs and updates old tasks — closed, pending, suspended or in progress.
02

Validation

The gatekeeper before costly execution.

  • Extracted decisions are checked for feasibility across infrastructure, network and database architectures.
  • Rigid requirements — such as SAP customization steps — are validated, instantly flagging compliance risks.
  • Company-specific design standards and regulations become automated boundaries every task must clear.
  • Non-compliant tasks are stopped before they ever reach the execution phase.
03

Telemetry

Schedule and human signals, continuously.

  • Tasks are projected against the master timeline; changed dates and schedule slippage are flagged automatically.
  • Predictive resource forecasting scans weeks and months ahead to surface bottlenecks early.
  • Workload is aggregated per person, triggering an early-warning alarm near burnout thresholds.
  • Friction at task hand-offs is detected, with actionable team-management and coaching suggestions.

Inside the cockpit

Capabilities that keep the project state honest.

Action-item extraction & owner binding

Turns raw dialogue into discrete tasks and assigns each one to the right attendee — no manual note-taking.

Historical reconciliation

Cross-references new discussion against previous backlogs and updates the state of older tasks automatically.

Schedule slippage detection

Verifies deliverables against the baseline; when a date pushes past a deadline, the slippage is flagged at once.

Predictive resource forecasting

Calculates cumulative task load across the enterprise weeks ahead, so managers see bottlenecks before delivery is hit.

Compliance & feasibility validation

Filters decisions through infrastructure, network, database and SAP-customization rules, forming automated boundaries.

Workload & burnout telemetry

Tracks individual workload and efficiency, raising early warnings and suggesting coaching before people burn out.

The output

A live, company-wide PMO dashboard.

Zooming out from the data layers reveals a centralized, continuously updated PMO dashboard — company-wide visibility across every project stage. It generates high-level initial project documentation automatically, then highlights exactly where humans need to step in with detail. Instead of relying on snapshots from days or weeks ago, the project's risk profile stays synchronized with the actual conversation.
