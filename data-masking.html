The problem

Sensitive data shouldn't slow you down — or leak.

GDPR and KVKK have sharply raised the bar on data-security audits across the EU and Turkey. Most SAP landscapes still rely on static access rules that either lock down sensitive fields too aggressively, slowing operations, or leave them exposed to unauthorized access — risking fines and reputational damage.

Why GNDLF?

From static restriction to dynamic, audited protection.

GNDLF Data Masking protects sensitive SAP data end-to-end — from the moment it's created, to the moment it's reported, to the moment it's archived — balancing compliance, flexibility and accountability.

01

Compliance by Design

Built directly into SAP, not bolted on afterward.

  • Full SAP Data Protection Masking (DPM) coverage, aligned to GDPR and KVKK.
  • Supports compliance across every process, not just one module.
  • Unifies protection across every SAP interface, not a single transaction.
  • Strengthens protection of sensitive data without breaking existing workflows.
02

Dynamic Protection

Context-aware access rules, not rigid field-level locks.

  • Dynamic Data Masking (DDM) with contextual authorization — not blanket restriction.
  • UI Data Protection Masking & Logging on every Fiori-based screen.
  • Meets legal and regulatory requirements without slowing operations.
  • Minimizes financial and reputational risk while preserving flexibility and accountability.
03

Lifecycle Governance

Covers data from creation to archiving, not just the database.

  • SAP Test Data Migration Server (TDMS) for high-speed, privacy-safe data transport.
  • Full client-specific data transfer, with company-code and time savings.
  • SAP Information Lifecycle Management (ILM) balances cost, risk and compliance.
  • Defines data governance policies and manages archiving end-to-end.
